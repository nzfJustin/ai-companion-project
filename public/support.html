Frequently asked questions

What is Ami?

Ami is an AI companion for reflecting on your day. You chat with it, and when you end a conversation, Ami writes a short memory so it can remember what matters to you next time. The Trends screen shows how your mood has changed over time.

Is Ami a therapist?

No. Ami is an AI and doesn't provide therapy or medical advice. It can be a helpful place to think out loud, but for mental-health support please talk to a qualified professional.

Who can see my conversations?

Your messages and memories are encrypted when stored. To generate replies, your conversation is processed by our AI provider, Anthropic. We don't sell your data or show ads. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do memories work?

Tap End at the top of a conversation. About a minute later, a memory summarizing it appears under Memories.

How do I turn the daily reminder on or off? (iPhone)

Go to Settings → Reminders → Daily check-in reminder and choose a time. You can also turn off notifications for Ami in iOS Settings.

How do I change Ami's tone?

Go to Settings → Communication style.

I can't sign in.

Check that you're using the email you signed up with. If it still doesn't work, email us from that address and we'll help.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. This permanently deletes your account and your conversations, memories, and other data, as described in the Privacy Policy. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Email us. Screenshots help!
