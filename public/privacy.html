Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

Ami is an AI companion for reflecting on your day. This policy explains what information Ami collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Ami iOS app and the Ami web app at app-ami.com (together, "Ami").

Ami is operated by Justin Nie, an individual developer based in California, USA ("we", "us"). Questions? Email niezifan95@gmail.com.

The short version: we collect what you type to Ami so it can reply and remember what matters to you. Your messages are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate replies. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising SDKs. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address, display name, and password. We store only a secure one-way hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Preferences: your communication style (for example, "Warm") and timezone.
  • Conversations: the messages you send to Ami and Ami's replies.
  • Memory PIN (optional): if you set one to protect sensitive memories, we store only a hash of it.

Information Ami creates from your conversations

  • Memories: short summaries of past conversations, with a title, date, and sensitivity level.
  • Emotion tags: labels such as "calm" or "joy" that Ami assigns to conversations and messages. These power the Trends screen.
  • Context: a running summary of things you've told Ami (for example, goals you've mentioned) so it can keep track across conversations.
  • Usage information: conversation counts, check-in streaks, and timestamps (when a conversation started and ended).

Technical information

  • Our servers keep operational logs (a request ID, your account ID, which feature was used, whether it succeeded, and how long it took). These logs do not contain your message content.
  • Our hosting providers may process your IP address and standard request information to deliver the service and protect it from abuse.

Information stored only on your device

  • In the iOS app, your sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain, and your daily reminder setting is stored on your device. Daily reminders are scheduled by your iPhone; we don't receive them.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or advertising identifiers. Ami contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To generate Ami's replies, memories, emotion tags, and trends.
  • To run your account: signing you in, keeping you signed in, and saving your preferences.
  • To keep Ami safe and reliable: preventing abuse, enforcing usage limits, and fixing problems.
  • To show crisis resources if a conversation suggests you may be at risk.

We do not sell your personal information, use it for advertising, or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not use your conversations to train our own AI models.

3. AI processing

Ami's replies and memories are generated by Claude, an AI model provided by Anthropic, PBC. To do this, the relevant parts of your conversation (and the context Ami keeps about you) are sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic processes this data to provide the service to us under its commercial terms. Those terms say Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models, and that it may keep that data for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring. See Anthropic's privacy information.

Ami is an AI. It can be wrong, and it is not a therapist, doctor, or crisis service.

5. Security

All traffic between your device and Ami is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Your messages and memory summaries are also encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM with a key specific to your account. These keys are derived from a secret held on our servers. This is not end-to-end encryption: our servers can decrypt your data in order to provide the service (for example, to send it to the AI provider). Access is restricted, but no system is perfectly secure.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your account and the data associated with it, including conversations, memories, emotion data, and context, within 30 days. Copies in our providers' backups may remain for a limited time until they are overwritten. Operational logs that contain no message content may be kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: in the app, go to Settings → Delete account. You can also email us to ask.
  • Access or correct: you can view your conversations, memories, and trends in the app and change your preferences in Settings. Email us for a copy of your data or other requests.
  • Notifications: daily reminders are off by default. You can turn them off in Ami's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to know, access, correct, and delete your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We don't sell or share personal information as those laws define it. To make a request, email us. We'll verify it comes from your account's email address and respond within the time the law requires.

8. Age requirement

Ami is for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Where your data is processed

Ami is operated from the United States, and your information is processed and stored in the United States by us and our service providers.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we'll also let you know in the app before they take effect.
