## Description (4000 max)

Ami is an AI companion for the end of your day: a calm place to talk things through, and a friend who actually remembers.

TALK IT THROUGH
Tell Ami about your day: what went well, what's on your mind, what you're looking forward to. Ami listens, asks thoughtful questions, and replies in a warm, natural voice. Pick the communication style that suits you.

IT REMEMBERS WHAT MATTERS
When you end a conversation, Ami writes a short memory of it. Next time, Ami already knows the people, plans, and moments you've mentioned, so you never have to start from scratch.

SEE HOW YOU'RE DOING
Each conversation gets a gentle mood tag, like calm or joy. Trends shows how your days have felt over time, and your check-in streak keeps the habit going.

A DAILY CHECK-IN, IF YOU WANT ONE
Turn on an optional daily reminder at the time that works for you. It's off unless you choose it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your messages and memories are encrypted when stored
• No ads, no tracking, and we never sell your data
• Delete your account and data any time, right in the app

Ami is an AI, not a therapist or medical service, and it can make mistakes. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 (US) or contact your local emergency services.

Ami is for adults 18+.
